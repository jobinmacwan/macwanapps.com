How it works

From first lesson to test day

1

Set your date and province

Add your test date and where you live. The app counts down, sets a daily pace and adds the questions about your province or territory.

2

Learn one chapter at a time

All ten chapters of Discover Canada as short, two-minute lessons. Read them, or listen with the screen off.

3

Practise with real explanations

Every question tells you why the answer is right and which part of the guide it comes from, so mistakes turn into knowledge.

4

Pass the mock exam

Take full 20-question mock exams in the online or in-person format until you pass comfortably — then walk in ready.

Study by chapter

Bite-size lessons for every chapter

Discover Canada, rewritten into clear lessons of about two minutes each. See what you've read and mastered, practise that chapter on its own, and review only the questions you got wrong.

Every answer explained

Learn why, not just what

Answer a question and see right away whether you got it, with a plain-language explanation and the section of the official guide it comes from. Save the tricky ones to revisit later.

Listen anywhere

Every lesson, read aloud

Narration in English and Canadian French, with the text lighting up as it's read. Change the speed, skip back 15 seconds, and keep listening on the commute or from the lock screen.

Mock exams

The real test, before the real test

20 questions and a pass mark of 15, timed like the online (45 minutes) or in-person (30 minutes) test. Nothing is revealed until you submit — then you get your score and a chapter-by-chapter breakdown.

Know when you're ready

A readiness score you can trust

Mastery, coverage and mock-exam results roll into one readiness score, and a 12-week activity map keeps your streak honest. Your test date sets the daily pace.
