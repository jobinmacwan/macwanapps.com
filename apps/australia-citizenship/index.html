How it works

From first lesson to test day

1

Set your test date

Add the day of your appointment. The app counts down and sets a daily pace that gets you there, with study reminders if you want them.

2

Learn the four testable Parts

34 short lessons cover Parts 1 to 4 of Our Common Bond, the only parts the test draws from. The booklet itself is free to read in the app.

3

Practise with explanations

Every question tells you why the answer is right and which booklet section it was checked against. Questions you've seen are set aside, so practice doesn't repeat.

4

Pass the mock exam

20 questions, 45 minutes, 15 to pass and all five values questions right, scored exactly the way the real test scores you.

The values questions

Five questions you can't get wrong

Every applicant must answer all five Australian values questions correctly, whatever their overall score. A dedicated drill, written from Part 4 of the booklet, shows you how these scenario questions are worded until five out of five is routine.

Mock exams

The real test, before the real test

Twenty questions, three options each, and a 45-minute clock. Flag questions as you go and review everything before you submit. Nothing is revealed until you do, just like the real thing.

Both results

See the score the real test would give you

Your overall score and your values score, side by side, with a chapter-by-chapter breakdown and the reason if either falls short.

Every answer explained

Learn why, not just what

Answer a question and see straight away whether you got it, with a plain-language explanation and the booklet section it comes from. Save the tricky ones, and review your mistakes until they stick.

Why it's different

Built to be trusted on test day

Facts that are actually current

Governor-General, Prime Minister, premiers, seat counts: every fact that can change is verified, shown with the date it was last checked, and updated without waiting for a new app version.

Only what's tested

Parts 1 to 4 of the booklet, nothing else. No population figures and no trivia from the parts of the booklet the test doesn't cover.

Assisted Test, too

Approved for the Assisted Test? One switch gives the mock exam 90 minutes instead of 45.

No ads, no subscription trap

Part 1, the values drill and two mock exams are free. Full access is an in-app purchase. No ads, ever.

Private by design

No account and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your iPhone, and usage analytics are anonymous.

Works offline

Every question, lesson and the booklet are built in. Study on the train, on a plane, anywhere.
