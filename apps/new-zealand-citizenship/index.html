NZ Citizenship Test · In development

Get ready for New Zealand's new citizenship test

From late 2027, most people applying for New Zealand citizenship by grant will need to pass a test. We're building a study app for it, and it will launch once the official study material is published.

What it covers

The topics, as announced

Expected topics

The Bill of Rights Act and human rights, voting rights and democratic principles, New Zealand's system of government, Te Tiriti o Waitangi, some criminal offences, and travelling overseas on a New Zealand passport.

Not published yet

The official study material, the exact start date, test locations and the fee per attempt. The Department of Internal Affairs says it will provide study material before the test becomes a requirement. We'll build every question against it, so the app launches after it does. Nobody can sell you an accurate practice test for New Zealand before then.

The app

What the app will include

Planned

Questions checked against the official material

Original practice questions written from the government's study material once it's published, each with an explanation and the section it comes from.

Planned

Mock tests in the real format

Timed mock tests that match the official format: currently announced as 20 multiple-choice questions with 15 needed to pass.

Planned

Short, clear lessons

Every topic explained in plain English, from the Bill of Rights Act to how Parliament works and what Te Tiriti o Waitangi means today.

Planned

Know when you're ready

A readiness score built from your accuracy, coverage and mock results, and a daily pace set by your test date.

Planned

Private by design

No account and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your iPhone, like our other citizenship apps.

Planned

Works offline

Every question and lesson built in, so you can study anywhere, with or without a signal.
