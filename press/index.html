Studio boilerplate

Macwan Apps is an indie iOS development studio founded in March 2025 by Jobin Macwan, a solo developer who has been building iOS apps since 2017. The studio ships consumer apps focused on building better habits — with clean native design, on-device privacy, and no ads. Its flagship app is WalkFirst, which blocks distracting apps until you reach your daily step goal.

Founder bio

Jobin Macwan is an iOS developer since 2017. After eight years and 15+ apps shipped in-house — including official EV-charging apps used across India — he went independent in March 2025 to build his own apps under Macwan Apps. He designs, codes, markets, and supports every app himself. More at his profile page.

Fast facts

  • Founded: March 2025, by a solo developer
  • Apps shipped: WalkFirst, with more in development
  • Business model: subscriptions and one-time purchases — no ads, no data selling
  • Privacy: WalkFirst is fully on-device; no accounts required in any app
  • Contact: support@macwanapps.com

The apps

WalkFirst – Earn Screen Time

WalkFirst turns daily steps into screen time: pick the apps that distract you, set a step goal, and they stay blocked until you've walked enough. Built on Apple's Screen Time (FamilyControls) framework and Apple Health — steps never leave the device. App Store · Product page
